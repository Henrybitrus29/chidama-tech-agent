# Pricing guide

All prices are estimates before any incentives. A final fixed quote is prepared only after a site assessment.

## Typical residential system prices
A 5 kW system typically costs between $13,500 and $16,000 installed. An 8 kW system typically costs between $20,000 and $24,500. A 12 kW system typically costs between $29,000 and $35,000. Residential systems usually work out to roughly $2.70 to $3.10 per watt.

## What changes the price
Roof complexity, the panel brand tier you choose and whether you add a battery all affect the price. If your electrical panel needs an upgrade to carry the system, expect an additional $1,500 to $3,000.

## Site assessment and quotes
The site assessment is free and carries no obligation. Quotes are valid for 30 days from the date they are issued.

## Incentives
Incentives and tax rules change over time. A Brightside advisor explains what applies to you when your quote is prepared. We do not give tax advice.
