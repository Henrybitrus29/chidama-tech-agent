# Warranty

## Equipment warranties
Solar panels carry a 25-year performance warranty that guarantees at least 87 percent of rated output in year 25. String inverters are covered for 12 years and microinverters for 25 years. Battery units are covered for 10 years.

## Workmanship and roof
Our installation workmanship is guaranteed for 10 years. Any leak caused by our roof penetrations is also covered for 10 years.

## Transfers
Warranties transfer to the next owner if you sell your home. Tell us about the sale so we can update our records.

## Making a claim
Call our support line or email us with your address and a description of the problem. We visit within 3 business days for warranty issues. The warranty does not apply if someone other than Brightside modifies the system.
