# How installation works

## Step 1: free consultation
A 20-minute phone call to understand your electricity use, your roof and your goals.

## Step 2: site assessment
A designer visits for about 45 minutes. They check the roof, the electrical panel and shading. The homeowner or another decision-maker should be present.

## Step 3: design and quote
You receive the system design and a fixed quote within 5 business days after the assessment.

## Step 4: permits
We prepare and file the city permit and the utility interconnection application for you. Permitting typically takes 3 to 6 weeks.

## Step 5: installation
A residential installation usually takes 1 to 2 days on site.

## Step 6: inspection and activation
After installation the city inspects the system and the utility gives permission to operate. This usually takes 1 to 3 weeks. Do not switch the system on before you receive that permission.

## Overall timeline
From a signed contract to an active system, plan on 6 to 10 weeks.
