# Appointments and policies

## Site assessments
Assessments are available Monday to Friday between 9:00 and 17:00 and on Saturday between 10:00 and 14:00. Each assessment lasts about 45 minutes.

## Rescheduling and cancelling
You can reschedule or cancel for free with at least 24 hours notice. After two missed appointments we ask you to rebook by phone.

## Privacy
We use your contact details only to reply to your enquiry and to prepare your quote. We do not sell your information.
