# Maintenance and support

## Cleaning
Panels need very little care. Rain is usually enough. In dusty areas we recommend a professional cleaning once a year.

## Monitoring
Every system includes a monitoring app that shows daily production. If a system stops reporting for 24 hours, our monitoring team contacts you.

## Power outages
A standard grid-connected system shuts down automatically during a blackout for safety. Only a system with a battery and backup wiring can power your home during an outage.

## Storms and hail
After severe hail or a storm, check the panels from the ground for visible damage and contact us. Do not climb on the roof.

## Support hours
The monitoring support line is answered seven days a week from 8:00 to 20:00.
