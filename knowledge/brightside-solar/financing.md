# Financing and payment

## Paying cash
A cash contract is paid in three stages: 10 percent when you sign, 40 percent when the equipment is delivered and 50 percent after the final inspection.

## Solar loans
We work with partner lenders who offer loans with terms of 10, 15 or 20 years. Interest rates depend on your credit and are set by the lender, not by Brightside. Checking your options uses a soft credit check that does not affect your score.

## Leases and power purchase agreements
We do not offer solar leases or power purchase agreements.
