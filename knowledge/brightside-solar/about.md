# About Brightside Solar

Brightside Solar designs and installs rooftop solar systems for homes and small businesses. The company has operated since 2014 and employs 38 people.

## Service area
We install in Harris, Fort Bend and Galveston counties in Texas. We do not travel outside these counties. We install rooftop systems on houses and on small commercial buildings up to 100 kW. We do not install ground-mounted systems or solar trackers, and we do not work on apartment buildings.

## Our team
All installations are done by our own in-house crews. We do not use subcontractors. System designers hold NABCEP certification.

## Business hours
We are open Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 17:00 and on Saturday from 10:00 to 14:00, Central Time. We are closed on Sundays and on federal holidays.
